# Personal Hero + Media Proof QA Contract

## Test A - Hero tells the personal transformation without losing actionability
Viewport: mobile 375x667 and desktop 1280x800

Steps:
1. Open the Hebrew homepage at scroll position 0.
2. Confirm the headline contains `מילד מוכה לחייל מצטיין` and the second line contains `מהישרדות — לאחריות`.
3. Confirm owner credit is visible near the hero media.
4. Confirm primary conversion actions remain usable and the hero does not become a text wall.
5. Repeat in English and Russian.

Expected:
- HE/EN/RU all communicate the same transformation arc.
- The hero remains visually dominated by Igor, not by UI chrome.
- Contact/evidence actions remain present and tappable.
- No direct quote marks are used for editorial bridging copy.

## Test B - Media proof appears immediately after the hero
Viewport: mobile 375x667 and desktop 1280x800

Steps:
1. Scroll one viewport below the hero.
2. Confirm `#media-proof` appears before the deeper documentary chapters.
3. Confirm at least 8 separate cards are present: owner archive, service archive, mynet, News 13, News 12, Channel 14, fatherhood coverage, Mindset.
4. On mobile, swipe horizontally through the cards.
5. On desktop, verify no collage overlay covers the hero portrait.

Expected:
- Media proof is an independent editorial surface.
- Mobile uses separate swipeable cards.
- Desktop uses large individual frames rather than a collage pasted onto the hero.

## Test C - Every visual carries attribution and source continuity
Viewport: desktop 1280x800

Steps:
1. Inspect each media proof card.
2. Confirm each card shows publisher/source context and a visible credit line.
3. Confirm mynet is credited to `קובי קואנקס`.
4. Confirm owner items use `© Igor Vepretski / 7YA` attribution.
5. Open the source CTA on mynet, News 13, News 12, Channel 14, Hidabroot and Mindset cards.

Expected:
- Every card has visible attribution.
- Source CTA opens the intended source in a new tab.
- Broadcast thumbnails are labeled as frames from the publisher/source, not as owner photography.
- No source-specific photograph is credited to Igor unless it is owner archive material.

## Test D - External image failure degrades safely
Viewport: mobile 375x667

Steps:
1. Block one Google Drive thumbnail request and one external publisher image request.
2. Reload the homepage.
3. Inspect the affected cards.

Expected:
- Cards retain readable title, source, quote and credit.
- Failed images fall back without an infinite onError loop.
- A media failure never removes the entire proof rail.

## Test E - Claim hygiene

Steps:
1. Inspect all quotation marks inside the media-proof component.
2. Verify quoted phrases are source-bound and short.
3. Verify editorial phrases such as `לפני החשיפה היה שירות` and `העבר לא מוצג כאן כמדליה. הוא מסביר את הדרך.` are presented as authored editorial framing, not falsely attributed to a publisher/interview.
4. Confirm no new reach, views, engagement or impact metrics are added by this component.

Expected:
- Direct quotes are traceable to the linked source or authored owner material.
- Editorial copy is distinguishable from publisher quotations.
- No metric inflation or class mixing is introduced.
