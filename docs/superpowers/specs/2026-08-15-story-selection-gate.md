# 7YA Story Selection Gate

**Date:** 2026-08-15  
**Status:** Approved operating rule from Igor Vepretski  
**Applies to:** homepage journey, chapter evidence, Proof of Movement, public archive promotion, Companion context

## Principle

The archive is comprehensive; the experience is selective.

7YA must never dump the full Drive, social archive or evidence ledger into the visitor journey. The system chooses public-safe stories that are narratively strong, cognitively accessible and useful inside the visitor's transformation path.

A source may be historically important and still remain in the deep archive if it does not improve the current chapter experience.

## Selection order

Every candidate story passes these gates in order:

1. **PUBLIC / SAFE** — source is public or can be described publicly without exposing private evidence, minors, credentials, private correspondence, legal/financial material or third-party sensitive data.
2. **STORY** — there is a human sequence: situation/tension → choice/action → consequence/response → meaning.
3. **ACCESSIBLE** — a first-time visitor can understand why it matters without prior knowledge of Igor, Israeli institutions or platform history.
4. **JOURNEY FIT** — the story advances exactly one major life chapter and helps move the visitor from Igor's experience toward their own reflection/action.
5. **EVIDENCE** — at least one strong source proves the central factual claim; interpretation is visibly separated from proof.
6. **VISUAL / MEDIA VALUE** — authentic photo, video, post capture, article, audio or document can carry the story without generic filler imagery. This improves ranking but is not mandatory.
7. **INFLUENCE SIGNAL** — public response, redistribution, discussion, adoption or declared action exists. This strengthens a story but must not be confused with causal proof.
8. **NON-REDUNDANCY** — if another story teaches the same lesson more clearly, the stronger story is promoted and the other remains available in the archive.

## Story score

Candidates may be ranked internally on a 100-point scale:

- Public safety / publishability — mandatory pass, then 0–10 quality
- Narrative strength — 0–20
- Accessibility — 0–15
- Journey relevance — 0–20
- Evidence strength — 0–15
- Authentic visual/media value — 0–10
- Public influence/reception signal — 0–10

A high numeric score does not override a privacy, truth or claim-control failure.

## Chapter grammar

Promoted stories should fit the site's canonical grammar:

`LIFE → EVIDENCE → MEANING → YOU`

For influence stories, extend it to:

`LIFE / ORIGINAL SIGNAL → EVIDENCE → PUBLIC MOVEMENT → MEANING → YOU`

The visitor should never need to understand the archive architecture in order to understand the story.

## Promotion levels

### A — Journey Hero Story
Use sparingly: one dominant story or transition per chapter. It must be emotionally legible, strongly sourced and central to the chapter's meaning.

### B — Supporting Story
One or two additional examples that deepen or complicate the chapter without derailing flow.

### C — Evidence Drawer / Proof of Movement
Useful proof, repost chains, metrics, reactions and secondary media. Available on demand, not forced into the main reading flow.

### D — Deep Archive Only
Valid public record that is redundant, too technical, too context-dependent, weakly visual or not useful to the current journey.

### Q — Quarantine
Private, conflicting, unsupported, identity-ambiguous or sensitive material. Never rendered publicly until resolved.

## Canonical examples of the intended method

- Early adversity / 2011 media anchor can establish origin because it is human, public, understandable and independently published.
- The fatherhood story can be promoted when the chapter is about presence/responsibility because it has a clear human tension, cross-platform distribution and public reception.
- Immigration / 'Russian insult' material can support belonging and identity because it connects lived experience to public language across platforms.
- StartOn is a major hero transition because it converts lived hardship into infrastructure for others and is supported by independent media plus owned institutional evidence.
- A large spreadsheet, metric snapshot or duplicated forensic workbook is evidence infrastructure, not a story by itself.
- A weak like/reaction may remain part of the influence graph without being promoted into the narrative unless it helps prove a broader response pattern.

## Visitor-first tie-breaker

When two public-safe stories are equally strong, choose the one that gives the visitor a clearer bridge into their own life, decision or next action.

Igor's biography is the evidence substrate; visitor development is the product outcome.

## Archive rule

No candidate is deleted merely because it is not promoted. Selection controls presentation, not preservation. The deep archive remains append-only and source-backed.
