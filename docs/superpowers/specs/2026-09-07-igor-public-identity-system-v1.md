## 1. Decision

Igor Vepretski will be presented as one coherent public entity across 7YA and every social surface. The public experience must feel like entering one system, not a collection of unrelated profiles, projects or design eras.

The master model is:

**IGOR → STORY → PROOF → IMPACT → WORK → NOW → ACTION**

7YA is the source of truth and public operating system. Social platforms are distribution surfaces. StartOn is a mission/project layer. Political, security, civic, media and creative activity are evidence-backed facets of the same person rather than separate brands.

## 2. Non-negotiable data integrity contract

No visual or editorial uplift may weaken provenance.

Every material public claim must be classified as one of:

1. **VERIFIED** — supported by a primary or reliable public source, timestamped record, original document, platform metric or directly inspectable source.
2. **OWNER-REPORTED** — provided by Igor but not independently verified to the same standard. Must be labeled when material.
3. **NARRATIVE / INTERPRETATION** — meaning, framing or editorial synthesis. Never rendered as a measured fact.
4. **UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED** — not published as fact until resolved.

Rules:

- Never convert cumulative reach or historical impact snapshots into mathematically additive totals unless the underlying datasets support it.
- Never present party activity as an official title unless supported by a primary source.
- Never imply an electoral conversion, vote contribution, partnership, employment relationship or institutional endorsement without evidence.
- Every metric card must be able to expose date, scope, source and calculation note one interaction deeper.
- Missing data is visible as missing; it is never replaced by AI estimation.

### 5.1 Visual token families

Exact final values require design-system implementation, but the token families are fixed:

- `surface.primary` — deep charcoal / near black
- `surface.secondary` — editorial dark gray
- `text.primary` — high-contrast off-white
- `text.secondary` — muted neutral
- `signal.primary` — electric/civic blue
- `signal.attention` — restrained amber/gold
- `signal.verified` — verification green used only for evidence state
- `signal.error` — reserved red for errors/warnings, not brand decoration

Typography hierarchy:

- `display` — cinematic editorial hero
- `headline` — strong compact authority
- `body` — high readability in HE/EN/RU
- `data` — tabular/metric labels
- `source` — small but legible provenance metadata

## 6. Public content taxonomy

Every piece of content must map to one primary pillar and optional secondary pillars.

Primary pillars:

1. **LIFE** — biography, family-safe personal history, turning points, resilience.
2. **PUBLIC** — civic and political participation, leadership, public commentary.
3. **SECURITY** — service, public safety, systems and governance experience.
4. **STARTON** — youth, technology, social innovation and organizational work.
5. **IMPACT** — source-backed metrics, distribution, public response and outcomes.
6. **MEDIA** — interviews, podcasts, broadcasts, collaborations, music and appearances.
7. **LIVE RECORD** — current events, active projects, recent milestones and source-backed updates.

A content item without a pillar, purpose, source state and CTA does not enter the canonical publishing system.

## 10. Political / public-life event model

Major public turning points should enter the canonical record as event objects, not temporary social posts.

Example model for 2026-09-06:

- candidacy context;
- documented committee process;
- official non-selection communication;
- public attendance at the list launch;
- subsequent public statements;
- measurable public response where available;
- later opportunities or consequences only when evidenced.

The system may show momentum over 24h / 72h / 7d / 30d, but must never infer political support from raw engagement alone.

## 11. Measurement layer

Metricool account currently exists but the observed brand object does not yet expose connected network data in `networksData`.

Therefore current policy is:

- do not claim live cross-platform telemetry until the relevant networks are connected and returning data;
- once available, store periodic snapshots with source and time window;
- measure views, reach, interactions, shares, saves, profile visits, follower delta and content velocity where the platform exposes them;
- track owned, earned and third-party distribution separately;
- maintain 24h, 72h, 7d and 30d views for major public moments.
