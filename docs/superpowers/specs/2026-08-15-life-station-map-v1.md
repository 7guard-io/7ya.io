# 7YA Life Station Map v1 — Igor Vepretski

**Date:** 2026-08-15  
**Status:** Content architecture approved for planning  
**Governing law:** `Evidence × Story × Art`  
**Primary interpretation signal:** interaction power across owned and external distribution  
**Public safety:** every station remains internal until its public evidence and privacy gate pass.

## 1. Purpose

This map expands the former seven-chapter biography into a granular living journey. The UI may group stations into chapters, but the data model must preserve the smaller stations so that the site can show real transitions rather than compress a life into résumé headings.

The system must learn importance from both biography and public interaction. A station becomes prominent when documented life significance intersects with measurable audience response, external redistribution, media pickup, institutional response or durable public memory.

The map is intentionally over-complete. It is easier to merge adjacent stations in presentation than to recover lost nuance after the data model has flattened them.

## 2. Evidence states

Every station receives one internal state:

- `VERIFIED` — supported by a source already present in the canonical archive/ledger or another strong source.
- `SUPPORTED` — supported by multiple known records but requires a final source bridge before public rendering.
- `OWNER_REPORTED` — known from Igor's account or private archive; must not be presented as independently verified.
- `REQUIRES_CONFIRMATION` — plausible/known in working context but exact date, wording or public-safe evidence still needs resolution.

The state controls publication wording; it does not decide whether the station matters.

## 3. Interaction-power model

For a station `s`, keep separate raw components rather than immediately collapsing them into one vanity score:

`Exposure(s)` — views, reach, impressions, profile visits where attributable.  
`DirectEngagement(s)` — likes/reactions, comments, saves, direct shares.  
`ExternalAmplification(s)` — reposts, mirrors, quote-posts, page pickups, journalist/editorial pickup.  
`SecondaryEngagement(s)` — views/reactions/comments/shares generated on the external copies when measurable.  
`CrossPlatformDiffusion(s)` — movement between TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, press, Telegram, blogs and other surfaces.  
`CrossLanguageDiffusion(s)` — Hebrew/Russian/English or other language migration.  
`Persistence(s)` — resurfacing, later references, archive survival after deletion or account loss.  
`Outcome(s)` — declared action, invitation, partnership, participation, institutional response or other attributable downstream result.

Interaction power means the whole distribution cascade, not comments alone.

## 4. Visual grammar per station

Each station may use one of these visual roles:

- `ORIGINAL_PHOTO` — authentic personal/public photo.
- `HERO_FRAME` — restored still from authentic video/interview/TV/podcast/lecture footage with source timestamp.
- `SOURCE_CAPTURE` — post, article, document or platform capture.
- `PROPAGATION_MAP` — original → republisher → secondary response → media/other platform.
- `DATA_MOMENT` — one meaningful metric or compact metric family attached to a source date.
- `OBJECT_MEMORY` — place, document, object, map or archival trace when no strong portrait exists.
- `EDITORIAL_COMPOSITION` — real weak source framed with typography/timeline/map/context without fabricating history.
- `ILLUSTRATIVE_ART` — clearly marked symbolic representation only when no authentic visual is available.

Important interviews, television appearances, public talks and video podcasts should preferentially use `HERO_FRAME`, not weak default thumbnails.

# 6. Presentation hierarchy

The 120 internal stations do **not** render as 120 equal cards. The runtime groups them into three presentation levels:

### Level A — Anchor Stations
Approximately 18–28 stations with the highest combination of life significance, source strength, interaction cascade and visual power. These receive full-screen or near-full-screen authored scenes.

### Level B — Bridge Stations
Approximately 35–55 stations that explain transitions, context or secondary impact. These render as compact story sequences, horizontal rails or expandable timeline nodes.

### Level C — Archive Stations
The remaining stations preserve chronology and evidence depth. They remain searchable/filterable in the archive and can be promoted when new evidence or interaction data increases their significance.

Promotion/demotion is data-driven but never automatic-publication.

# 8. Station ranking policy

A station receives separate normalized dimensions, each 0–100 only after source-normalization:

- `life_significance`
- `source_strength`
- `exposure_strength`
- `direct_engagement_strength`
- `external_amplification_strength`
- `secondary_engagement_strength`
- `cross_platform_strength`
- `cross_language_strength`
- `persistence_strength`
- `outcome_strength`
- `visual_strength`

For editorial ranking only, use:

`anchor_priority = 0.24*life_significance + 0.16*source_strength + 0.12*exposure_strength + 0.10*direct_engagement_strength + 0.14*external_amplification_strength + 0.06*secondary_engagement_strength + 0.05*cross_platform_strength + 0.03*cross_language_strength + 0.04*persistence_strength + 0.04*outcome_strength + 0.02*visual_strength`

The formula intentionally gives external redistribution more weight than raw likes and gives life significance the largest single weight. It is a curation aid, not a public “influence score.”

No station may receive a unique-people total unless deduplication across raw account/user identifiers is defensible.

# 9. User-facing filter axes

Deep exploration may filter the map by:

- period/year;
- life domain: origin / service / creator / fatherhood / StartOn / media / culture / politics / research / 7YA;
- evidence type;
- platform;
- language;
- interaction type;
- propagation depth;
- source status;
- visual medium;
- transformation theme: belonging / responsibility / voice / creation / repair / meaning / build.

The primary first-visit journey should not expose all these filters.

# 10. Acceptance rules

The life map is acceptable only when:

1. It can represent quiet biographical moments and high-scale viral moments without confusing importance with reach.
2. Views, likes, comments, shares, reposts, journalist/page pickups and their secondary engagement are first-class interaction data.
3. External republishing is represented as a graph edge, not merely a note.
4. Deleted/blocked accounts retain documented historical metrics and status.
5. Every major video/TV/interview node can reference an authentic source frame/timestamp.
6. No private Drive source becomes a public URL.
7. No generated visual is presented as historical evidence.
8. Minors/private family material remains protected.
9. The user can move from Igor's station to a reflection/action without turning the site into a questionnaire.
10. The system can add future stations without rewriting the entire chronology.

## Operating sentence

> **Do not summarize the life into a résumé. Reconstruct the transitions, prove the public signal, show how it travelled, and let the visitor discover what the journey makes possible for them.**
