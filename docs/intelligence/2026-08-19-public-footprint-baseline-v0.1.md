# 7YA Public Footprint Baseline v0.1

**Snapshot date:** 2026-08-19 (Asia/Jerusalem)  
**Purpose:** establish a quantitative floor for Igor Vepretski's public internet footprint so future scans can report delta, growth, propagation and evidence coverage rather than impressions.

## Measurement labels

- **MEASURED** — directly counted from a current or dated public/runtime source.
- **SOURCE-REPORTED** — a public first-party statement or analytics recap; counted but kept distinct from independently measured values.
- **INDEXED** — surfaced by public search/indexing or a third-party public index.
- **INFERRED** — model-derived relationship or propagation conclusion; never merged into measured totals without explicit methodology.

Policy: **measure aggressively; label precisely; apply the same standard to Igor and comparison subjects.**

## Next counting dimensions

1. `PUBLIC_OBJECT_COUNT` — distinct original posts/articles/videos/audio/research objects.
2. `DERIVATIVE_OBJECT_COUNT` — reposts, quote-posts, embeds, screenshots, mirrors and media reproductions.
3. `PLATFORM_COUNT` — distinct public platforms/domains.
4. `INTERACTION_FLOOR` — reactions + comments + shares visible in dated sources, never double-counting mirrored metrics.
5. `REACH_FLOOR` — only source-local measured/reported reach, with source and date.
6. `PROPAGATION_DEPTH` — maximum verified origin → repost → media → secondary-discussion chain depth.
7. `INDEPENDENT_PICKUP_COUNT` — distinct third-party publishers/accounts that republished, quoted or covered Igor-originated content.
8. `RESEARCH_OBJECT_COUNT` — manuscripts, working papers, essays, books, public research artifacts.
9. `PUBLIC_LEADERSHIP_OBJECT_COUNT` — political/public-policy posts, interviews, speeches, campaign/public-action records.
10. `CANON_COVERAGE_RATE` — proportion of discovered public objects represented in the canonical ledger.
11. `CONTRADICTION_COUNT` — materially conflicting public claims awaiting resolver treatment.
12. `STALE_FOOTPRINT_COUNT` — legacy pages that still rank/index with outdated claims or sensitive contact details.
