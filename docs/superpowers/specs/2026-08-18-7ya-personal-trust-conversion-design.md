# 7YA Personal Trust & Conversion Layer — Design Addendum

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Status:** Approved design direction
**Extends:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-18-7ya-living-universe-design.md`
**Governing sequence:** DISCOVER → VERIFY → RESOLVE → MAP → RANK → CURATE → BUILD → QA → PUBLISH

## Goal

Make 7YA feel maximally personal, direct, trustworthy and confidence-building while improving meaningful conversion. The site must not merely display Igor Vepretski; it must let a visitor understand him as a person, see evidence for material claims, understand why the work exists, and reach a relevant next action without encountering generic marketing pressure.

The design model is:

**PERSON → PROOF → PURPOSE → PARTICIPATION**

The intended emotional and cognitive progression is:

**Meet → Understand → Trust → Relate → Explore → Choose → Act.**

## Core rule

**Do not sell Igor. Let the visitor know him deeply enough that the value becomes self-evident.**

Conversion is a consequence of clarity, trust and relevance. It must not be manufactured by inflated claims, artificial urgency, fake social proof, manipulative personalization or repeated generic CTAs.

## 1. PERSON — Igor before brands

The home experience begins with the person before StartOn, 7YA, SUPERNOAH, political activity, research labels or institutional credibility.

The first screen should contain:

- one dominant authentic visual of Igor;
- `IGOR VEPRETSKI`;
- one concise first-person statement that expresses lived motive rather than résumé status;
- three high-value choices only:
  - enter the life story;
  - see what is happening now;
  - build / create / work together.

The first viewport must not lead with counters, logos, title stacks, service menus, partner walls, metric dashboards or a CV summary.

### Personal depth standard

Every major chapter should answer at least two of these questions where evidence and privacy permit:

- What happened to Igor?
- What did he feel / believe at the time?
- What did he do next?
- What did he publish or say publicly?
- What did other people or institutions do in response?
- What did he learn later?
- What did this become in his current work?

The site must move from biography to causality, not biography to self-promotion.

## 2. PROOF — confidence through evidence

Material public claims must be confidence-building because they are source-linked, not because the copy sounds authoritative.

Visible evidence states remain explicit:

- **VERIFIED** — direct supporting evidence exists;
- **OWNER-REPORTED** — Igor or an owned source reports the metric or claim;
- **INFERRED** — a plausible relationship is supported but not directly proven;
- **UNKNOWN** — evidence exists but consequence cannot be established;
- **DISPUTED** — evidence conflicts or reliability is insufficient;
- **PRIVATE / NOT FOR PUBLIC DISPLAY** — evidence may exist internally but must not be published raw.

Evidence value and public visual fitness are separate scores. A sensitive document may be high-value evidence while being unsuitable for public display.

### Proof placement

Evidence should appear adjacent to the relevant life moment or claim. Preferred bridge:

**Moment → source object → public echo → consequence / uncertainty.**

Do not force users into a separate evidence archive merely to verify the central story. Deep archive remains available for exhaustive inspection.

## 3. PURPOSE — show why each project exists

Projects must emerge from lived experience rather than appear as brand inserts.

Examples of required causal bridges:

- childhood / belonging → youth opportunity → StartOn;
- public systems / policing → institutional understanding → civic systems thinking;
- fatherhood / family responsibility → public writing on responsibility and presence;
- repeated public-content propagation → Influence Graph / evidence architecture;
- friction with bureaucracy around StartOn → entry into municipal / political action where verified;
- lived crisis / recovery → research, reflection and practical growth layers;
- creative history → music / media / CREATE WITH ME.

Where the causal relationship is Igor's interpretation rather than independently established fact, label it as present-day reflection or framework rather than objective causation.

## 4. PARTICIPATION — personal conversion, not a funnel

The visitor should encounter actions only after sufficient context. Calls to action are contextual handoffs, not persistent sales pressure.

### Primary visitor intents

The system may support these intent paths:

- **Understand the story** — continue through LIFE / chronology;
- **Work with Igor** — collaboration, advisory, public / civic, content or research conversation;
- **Invite Igor** — speaking, interview, podcast, media, workshop or event;
- **Build for young people** — StartOn / municipality / philanthropy / technology partnership;
- **Create together** — music, media, writing, digital campaign or collaborative production;
- **Study the work** — research, frameworks, SUPERNOAH, evidence architecture;
- **Grow personally** — practical reflection / Grow layer;
- **Explore the archive** — source-first deep dive.

The interface must never imply that all visitors need the same CTA.

### Contextual handoffs

Examples:

- after a StartOn chapter: `Build an opportunity pathway for young people`;
- after public influence: `Explore how the signal travelled` or `Build a public-information move together`;
- after research: `Read the source / framework` or `Discuss the research`;
- after creation: `Create with me`;
- after a personal chapter: `Continue the story` before any commercial ask.

Each major surface should have at most one dominant next action and one secondary exploration action.

## 5. Session-level personalization

Personalization is allowed only when it improves relevance without surveillance or manipulation.

### Allowed

Use session-local interaction signals such as:

- rooms visited;
- chapter depth;
- explicit visitor choice;
- language;
- whether the visitor repeatedly opens research, StartOn, media, creation or collaboration material.

Use these signals to:

- prioritize the next relevant room;
- change the order of optional suggestions;
- preserve progress in the session;
- avoid repeating the same CTA;
- surface a relevant collaboration path.

### Not allowed

Do not use covert identity inference, sensitive-trait inference, political persuasion profiling, diagnostic claims, hidden scoring of a named individual, manipulative scarcity or opaque third-party behavioral tracking as a requirement for the experience.

The system should work well with personalization disabled.

## 6. Visual memory, not visual repetition

Every major chapter must have a distinct visual memory where authentic assets exist.

Prioritize:

- real life photographs;
- public-action imagery;
- StartOn / youth / field work;
- military / public-service retrospective imagery where public-safe;
- media / broadcast frames;
- public posts and source captures;
- civic / political public activity where evidence and publication rights allow;
- music / creation;
- research documents;
- behind-the-scenes material;
- personal-life imagery only when explicitly safe for publication.

### De-duplication rule

The same source event should not dominate multiple adjacent cards unless the repeated frames communicate materially different evidence. Three captures from one article are lower priority than three different life domains with comparable evidence quality.

No collage unless explicitly requested. No generated historical evidence. No generic portrait fallback presented as if it were event documentation.

## 7. Tone and copy

Copy should be first-person where Igor is reflecting on his own life, and evidence-language where the system is asserting facts.

### Preferred tone

- direct;
- emotionally literate without melodrama;
- confident without boasting;
- precise about uncertainty;
- human before institutional;
- short enough to preserve visual pacing.

### Avoid

- generic founder language;
- inflated superlatives;
- repeated `impact / visionary / leader` abstractions without proof;
- corporate mission copy where a lived story can explain the same point;
- deficit framing that turns hardship into spectacle;
- aggressive lead-capture language.

## 8. Trust architecture

Trust is a product feature.

The interface should make it easy to distinguish:

- Igor speaking now;
- historical source material;
- third-party reporting;
- platform metrics;
- archived screenshots;
- research evidence;
- Igor's original framework;
- AI-generated explanation by the 7YA Companion.

The 7YA Companion must identify itself as AI based on Igor's public / approved corpus and must not impersonate Igor.

Sensitive evidence remains private even when it strengthens internal confidence in the canon.

## 9. Conversion measurement

Do not optimize primarily for raw click-through rate.

Preferred measures:

- meaningful chapter completion;
- evidence opens;
- movement from LIFE to a relevant project / idea;
- deliberate collaboration intent;
- StartOn partnership intent;
- research/document opens;
- CREATE WITH ME intent;
- return exploration within the session;
- successful handoff to the correct contact / collaboration route.

A lower-volume but better-qualified action is superior to a high-volume generic click.

## 10. Home hierarchy

Recommended order after this addendum:

1. **PERSON** — dominant real-life hero and first-person motive;
2. **LIVED STORY** — first meaningful chapters, not an index;
3. **PROOF IN CONTEXT** — source objects attached to those chapters;
4. **RIGHT NOW** — what Igor is actually doing now;
5. **PUBLIC ECHO / INFLUENCE** — selected causal trails, not vanity totals;
6. **STARTON** — consequence of lived experience;
7. **THINK / RESEARCH** — intellectual depth with publication-status honesty;
8. **CREATE** — music, media, creative work and co-creation;
9. **YOUR PATH** — lightweight visitor intent / personal next step;
10. **ARCHIVE** — exhaustive exploration.

The page must not render all ten as equally heavy blocks. Editorial pacing determines how much of each is visible before deeper navigation.

## 11. Multilingual behavior

Hebrew, Russian and English remain cultural views of one canonical corpus.

Personal copy may be adapted culturally for emotional clarity, but facts, evidence states, dates, source relationships and verification boundaries must remain equivalent.

Commercial / collaboration language should sound natural in each language rather than being mechanically translated.

## 12. Safety and reversibility

Public, political, legal, financial, family and sensitive historical material must pass evidence and privacy gates before publication.

For significant irreversible public changes:

- preserve previous deployable version;
- confirm source / verification status;
- verify no private identifiers are exposed;
- perform desktop + mobile + HE/EN/RU QA;
- confirm key source links;
- verify the dominant CTA does not misrepresent the relationship or service being offered.

## 13. Success criteria

This layer passes only if a new visitor can answer, after a reasonable session:

1. Who is Igor as a person?
2. What shaped him?
3. What did he actually do?
4. Which claims can I verify?
5. Why did StartOn / 7YA / the research / public work emerge?
6. What is he doing now?
7. What part is relevant to me?
8. What can I do next?

### Personal QA

Ask after every major implementation slice:

> **Could this experience only belong to Igor Vepretski?**

If the names, images and logos can be swapped for another founder / influencer while the experience still makes sense, the slice is too generic and fails.

## Non-goals

This addendum does not authorize:

- a total visual rewrite unrelated to the Living Universe baseline;
- exposing private family material;
- fabricated reach totals;
- fake testimonials or social proof;
- creating unverifiable political achievements;
- building a CRM-heavy lead funnel on the public homepage;
- replacing authentic evidence with AI-generated imagery;
- turning every life event into a sales opportunity.

## First implementation slice

The first slice should be deliberately narrow and testable:

1. reweight the home opening around PERSON → PROOF → PURPOSE;
2. create one reusable contextual handoff component for PARTICIPATION;
3. apply it to three existing high-evidence journeys: StartOn, public influence and research / THINK;
4. add session-local intent memory with no sensitive profiling;
5. reduce repeated imagery in the first major visual wall by preferring distinct canonical life domains;
6. perform desktop + mobile + HE/EN/RU + evidence-boundary QA;
7. deploy with rollback preserved and verify production.

Later slices may expand this pattern through the full chronology after the first slice proves the experience.
