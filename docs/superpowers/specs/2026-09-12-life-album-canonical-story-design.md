# 7YA Canonical Life Album — Design

## Purpose
Make 7ya.io feel like walking through Igor Vepretski's life, not browsing a portfolio, evidence database, or social-media archive.

## Canonical Story Rule
Every life chapter is structured in this order:

AGE → PLACE → PEOPLE → MOMENT → ORIGINAL MEDIA → PUBLIC RESPONSE → CONSEQUENCE → REFLECTION

Evidence, provenance, counters and technical trust metadata support the story. They never lead it.

## Experience Principle
The primary homepage is a chronological personal album. The visitor should feel: “I am moving through Igor's life with him.”

A chapter answers eight questions:
1. How old was Igor?
2. Where was he?
3. Who mattered in that period?
4. What actually happened?
5. What original artifact survives from that moment?
6. What did people say/do in response?
7. What happened next because of it?
8. What does Igor understand about it now?

## Narrative Spine
The current spine is:
- Origin / childhood / immigration / neighborhood
- Service / security / police
- Fatherhood / family responsibility
- Return to Jesse Cohen / StartOn
- Identity / public voice / viral story families
- Creation / music / creator collaborations
- Public leadership / politics / 2026 rupture
- 7YA / current work / next move

Periods may overlap. Chronology remains explicit and age is always derived from DOB 1990-07-07.

## Media Rules
- Use Igor's authentic source media first.
- Never invent childhood/service photography.
- If no authentic visual exists, render an explicit source frame rather than synthetic imagery.
- Reposts/mirrors belong under the original Moment as Impact/Echo, not as independent “new stories.”
- Audience comments can appear when public-safe and source-linked.
- Metrics remain source-local and date-bound; no synthetic reach totals.

## Data / Truth Boundaries
Reuse the existing Content Core, Public Projection, media-impact ledger, deep-media registry and existing album/broadcast components. Do not create another database or parallel canon.

The homepage may include editorial metadata such as age/place/people/reflection, but factual claims must be source-grounded or clearly labeled as retrospective reflection.

## Homepage Composition
1. Personal cover / current portrait
2. Chronological life chapters
3. “Posts as Memory” story families
4. Full Broadcast: source objects, reposts, reactions, comments, consequences
5. Current work / StartOn / music / public leadership / research
6. Sources / evidence / archive as depth, not as opening experience

## Interaction
- Chapter rail includes age and period, not year alone.
- “What happened next?” may be used between major moments when the answer is documented.
- A visitor can open original media, comments, echoes and consequences without leaving the chronological context.
- Mobile is first-class; no horizontal page overflow and no tiny interaction targets.

## Safety / Integrity
- Family/children content is privacy-gated and minimized.
- Host/editor framing is never promoted to an unverified first-person biographical fact.
- Self-authored sources are labeled as such and not treated as independent verification.
- Weak/disputed material stays out of the public life spine until resolved.

## Success Criteria
A first-time visitor can scroll the homepage and understand Igor as a person across time without opening Evidence.

Each major period visibly includes at least one of: authentic photo, original post, video, document, public reaction, or source frame.

The strongest chapters show the complete chain:
MOMENT → ORIGINAL ARTIFACT → PEOPLE'S RESPONSE → DISTRIBUTION → CONSEQUENCE → PRESENT-DAY REFLECTION.

The site no longer needs the visitor to understand 7YA's internal systems to understand Igor.
