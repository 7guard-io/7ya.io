## Test 1 - Navigate the multilingual site with visible source media [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: canonical homepage, repaired deep-route navigation, HE/EN/RU switching, Visible Corpus, source-linked media
Description: Verifies the primary site journey stays rich and visible instead of becoming text-only or routing back to the homepage unexpectedly.

Steps:
1. Open the Hebrew homepage and use Story, Archive, Media and Research navigation in sequence.
2. On a deep page, inspect the Live Visual Corpus rail and open one source card.
3. Switch the current deep page to English and then Russian.

Expected: Homepage remains DocumentaryHome; Archive, Media and Research open their intended content; each deep page visibly contains multiple image/video/source cards near the top; the source opens; HE, EN and RU keep coherent translated headings and navigation without exposing an empty or generic page.
