# 7YA — DO NOT REPEAT

This file records failure modes already observed in prior 7YA work. Treat it as a preflight checklist for every future agent, designer, developer and automation.

## Content failures

- Do not invent moments, quotes, metrics, dates, thumbnails or public reactions.
- Do not use generic AI imagery to stand in for real events or people when authentic media exists.
- Do not present generated people as Igor Vepretski.
- Do not treat one platform snapshot as a full historical corpus.
- Do not merge owned reach with external amplification.
- Do not publish unsupported aggregate reach numbers.
- Do not turn political engagement evidence into endorsement or persuasion.
- Do not expose sensitive family, child, legal, financial, health, address or operational information.

## Experience failures

- Do not lead with dashboard language, release gates, verification jargon or internal architecture.
- Do not make the visitor learn the system before understanding Igor.
- Do not create duplicate navigation systems.
- Do not reduce the life story to job-title chronology.
- Do not hide StartOn as a minor portfolio card.
- Do not make the Evidence layer visually louder than the human story.
- Do not use dense collage as a substitute for editorial selection.
- Do not ship partial or mixed-language pages as complete locales.

## Engineering failures

- Do not edit an obsolete or parallel 7YA repository by mistake.
- Do not assume a commit means production changed.
- Do not deploy a stale or unverified build.
- Do not add another app-builder project as a new source of truth.
- Do not bypass preview and visual QA for redesign work.
- Do not claim completion before live-domain verification.
- Do not let copied ZIPs, temporary exports or chat memory override canonical source files.

## Required behavior

Before implementation:

1. inspect the canonical repository
2. inspect the live site
3. retrieve current source/content indexes
4. verify the relevant real media and evidence
5. work in a dedicated branch
6. validate desktop + mobile + HE/RU/EN before production promotion
