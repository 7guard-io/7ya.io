# 7YA Public Media Corpus Policy

## Purpose

7YA must learn and reuse Igor Vepretski's public media corpus comprehensively, without pretending to have access to content that is unavailable.

## Corpus scope

Include, where publicly accessible or available through owner-authorized exports/connectors:

- TikTok current and legacy accounts
- Instagram primary and secondary accounts
- Facebook current, personal and legacy/archive surfaces
- YouTube and Shorts
- LinkedIn posts and articles
- X
- Threads
- Telegram
- Snapchat and other discovered public accounts
- Spotify / Apple Music / music services
- podcasts and long-form audio
- television and interviews
- press and articles
- StartOn public media and documents
- Wikimedia / Medium / Academia / Linktree
- public mirrors, reposts and external distribution
- public comments and reactions where preserved or retrievable
- historical/legacy links and archived traces

## Coverage labels

Every source/platform must be labeled:

- `FULL`: owner export or equivalent complete enumeration has been parsed.
- `BEST_AVAILABLE`: all accessible connected/public evidence has been exhausted but full native enumeration is unavailable.
- `BLOCKED_GAP`: a known corpus exists but cannot currently be enumerated or verified completely.

## Reuse requirements

A media item may be reused when the system can identify enough provenance to avoid misrepresentation. Prefer:

1. original owned media
2. canonical original post
3. owner export / analytics snapshot
4. verified third-party publication/repost
5. public indexed evidence with clear limitations

Never synthesize missing provenance.

The Top 100 is an editorial selection layer, not the corpus itself.

## Editorial rule

Before selecting media for any page, chapter, carousel, pitch or homepage module:

1. retrieve the relevant canonical corpus/index
2. retrieve all candidate items in the matching story family or life era
3. deduplicate mirrors/reposts
4. distinguish owned from external distribution
5. prefer authentic media quality and story importance over recency alone
6. preserve source links and verification state
7. record why each selected item was chosen

## Documentary integrity

If authentic media is missing, use a source card, text/document frame or explicit gap. Do not manufacture a documentary moment with generated imagery.
