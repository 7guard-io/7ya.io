01 · BIO

Biography for editorial use

Short · about 70 words

Igor Vepretski is an Israeli social entrepreneur, creator and public-systems builder working across youth and technology, community, digital media and civic life. He founded StartOn and is building 7YA, an evidence-linked public archive and platform. His transition from a police and public-service background toward youth-focused social action was covered by mynet and Israel’s Channel 13.

Extended · about 160 words

Igor Vepretski works at the intersection of public systems, youth and technology, community and digital media. After years in security, policing and public service, he was profiled in 2022 by mynet and interviewed by Israel’s Channel 13 following his decision to move from enforcement toward building opportunities for young people at risk in Jesse Cohen, Holon. He founded StartOn, a registered Israeli nonprofit focused on education and technological opportunity for youth, and is building 7YA, a public platform that connects biography, media, research and evidence rather than leaving a public record scattered across platforms. Vepretski has also written publicly about education in the digital era and spoken about social-media influence on young people. Vepretski says he participated in Yisrael Beiteinu’s 2026 candidate-selection process for the 26th Knesset. He presents political activity as one arena within a broader focus on public leadership, social mobility and practical youth infrastructure.

05 · FACT POLICY

What editors can rely on

Third-party sources are preferred over owned sources. Internal data is labeled as internal. Reach and impression figures are not merged into a single cumulative “audience” number without an export, time window and method that prevents double counting. Institutional participation is not described as sponsorship, partnership, funding or endorsement unless a primary source supports that wording.

Suitable for use

  • Third-party coverage with a public URL and date.
  • StartOn and 7YA descriptions when owned-source status is clear.
  • An explicitly owner-reported description of participation in the 2026 candidate-selection process, without exposing private correspondence.
  • Participation by the StartOn team in a 2023 Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub Welcome Event, while explicitly not inferring sponsorship, partnership, funding or endorsement.
  • The CC0 Wikimedia Commons press image.

Withheld pending stronger verification

  • Cumulative claims of billions of views, reach or impressions.
  • Institutional “sponsorship” or “partnership” wording without a primary document.
  • Titles, ranks or dates that conflict across older records.
  • Reuse of broadcaster images or video frames without permission.
