# 7YA — Autobiographical Cinema Design

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: APPROVED DIRECTION / DESIGN SPEC FOR USER REVIEW
Owner: Igor Vepretski / 7YA

## 1. Problem

The current 7YA production contains substantial material — life chronology, public-source visuals, interviews, posts, StartOn, research, media, archives and the AI companion — but the homepage presents too much of that material as a system of sections, cards, ledgers, labels, statuses and indexes.

The result is informationally rich but emotionally weak. The visitor learns that Igor has a story instead of feeling that they are moving through it.

The redesign must reverse that hierarchy:

**Human experience first → motion and media second → public echo third → evidence and systems on demand.**

The public archive, verification discipline and source-local metrics remain intact, but they stop competing with the autobiographical experience.

## 2. North Star

7YA should feel less like a personal website and more like entering a living autobiographical film.

The visitor should experience:

> I lived something → I remember it → this is the real artifact from that world → I gave it a public voice → the world responded → this is what I understand now → this is what another person can do with it.

The site must be unmistakably Igor Vepretski without becoming self-congratulatory. Igor is the human thread; the purpose is to turn lived experience into responsibility, knowledge, opportunity and action for others.

The emotional thesis is:

**Pain → understanding → responsibility → building → opportunity for another person.**

## 3. Experience Principles

### 3.1 Person before system
The first minutes of the site should not lead with 7YA architecture, counts, evidence terminology, platform matrices or research categories. They should lead with Igor's face, voice, places, memories and turning points.

### 3.2 Scenes, not sections
The homepage becomes a sequence of cinematic scenes rather than a stack of independent product modules.

Each scene has one dominant emotional question and one dominant visual language. UI controls remain secondary.

### 3.3 Real source media first
Priority order for visuals:
1. authentic personal/archive photography approved for public use;
2. public press photography;
3. public broadcast/video frames;
4. original post screenshots or platform-native visuals;
5. clearly labeled editorial reconstruction only when authentic visual evidence is unavailable.

Generic stock is not used. Generated imagery must never be presented as documentary evidence.

### 3.4 Motion must express biography
Motion is not hover decoration. Scroll choreography should communicate change over time: arrival, rupture, service, becoming a father, public voice, propagation, return, building, creation, research and the unfinished present.

### 3.5 Evidence is a second layer
Source, date, status and metric remain accessible through compact evidence drawers, source affordances and deep pages. They do not dominate the main emotional frame.

### 3.6 One story, no duplicated warehouses
The same item should not be separately retold in a chronology card, media wall, impact grid and archive card on the homepage. A source belongs to its primary autobiographical moment; deeper indexes remain available elsewhere.

### 3.7 Leadership without vanity
The tone is forceful and responsible, not promotional. The site should communicate that public leadership grew from lived responsibility and system experience, not from a desire to accumulate titles.

## 4. The Homepage Narrative Arc

The canonical top-level sequence is:

**CHILD → OUTSIDER → BELONGING → SERVICE → RESPONSIBILITY → FATHER → VOICE → PUBLICATION → PUBLIC ECHO → BREAK / RECKONING → RETURN → STARTON → CREATION → RESEARCH → 7YA → NOW → YOU**

This is not necessarily one scene per word. Adjacent concepts may be combined when the resulting scene is stronger.

### Scene 00 — Cold Open / “I am still becoming”
- Full-viewport authentic portrait or moving source frame.
- Minimal interface.
- First-person line, not résumé copy.
- No statistics.
- No LIFE/ECHO/LAB/BUILD labels above the fold.
- Companion is visibly available but does not compete with the first emotional beat.
- Scroll initiates the story rather than jumping to a card grid.

Suggested Hebrew emotional direction:
> נולדתי במקום אחד. גדלתי במקום אחר. הרבה שנים ניסיתי להבין איפה אני שייך.
> בסוף הבנתי שאני לא מחפש רק מקום לעצמי. אני רוצה לבנות מקום לאחרים.

Final copy must be source-safe and editorially reviewed before publication.

### Scene 01 — Origin / Immigration / Belonging
Purpose: make the reader feel the early search for belonging before explaining later achievements.

Visual grammar:
- archival/place imagery where public-safe material exists;
- press source that retrospectively documents early life can appear as evidence, not as a fake childhood photo;
- geographic transitions may move from Kharkiv → Bat Yam → Holon / Jesse Cohen.

Narrative voice:
- first person;
- specific but restrained around other people;
- focus on what the experiences did to Igor rather than exposing third-party private details.

Underlying question:
**Who decides which child is a problem and which child is potential?**

### Scene 02 — Service / Responsibility
Purpose: transform service from résumé status into a lived school of responsibility.

Visual grammar:
- real public interview frames and safe public sources;
- disciplined, quieter motion; less spectacle;
- transitions from protection / observation / systems to responsibility for people.

The scene must not publish unresolved duration or role claims until verified. Where records conflict, the public copy uses only verified wording.

### Scene 03 — Fatherhood / Presence
Purpose: expose the internal cost and responsibility dimension before public leadership language.

Visual grammar:
- no exploitative exposure of children;
- use public-safe material, symbolic framing, authored posts and text where appropriate;
- the fatherhood post becomes part of this scene, not a detached viral card.

Narrative question:
**What does it mean to be present when ambition constantly pulls outward?**

### Scene 04 — Voice / The internet becomes a public arena
Purpose: show how writing, video, humor, music and public posts became a way to process life and participate in society.

This is the first major propagation scene.

Scroll choreography example:
1. private/lived context enters;
2. original post enters full frame;
3. post shrinks slightly as external reactions appear around it;
4. repost / publisher / article / broadcast nodes become visible;
5. a compact metric strip appears only after the user has seen the story;
6. source-local numbers remain separated by platform and date.

### Scene 05 — Public Echo / When a personal post leaves my hands
Purpose: make influence visible as movement rather than a dashboard.

Anchor candidates include:
- fatherhood / presence;
- identity and belonging;
- elder-fraud story leading to public media discussion;
- creator-scale / TikTok owner snapshots;
- other verified high-signal posts.

Each anchor follows:
**origin → publication → distribution → response → media pickup → verified consequence / unresolved boundary.**

No artificial total-reach number.

### Scene 06 — Break / Reckoning
Purpose: allow contradiction and difficulty to exist in the autobiography.

Possible editorial themes, only where publication is public-safe and evidence-supported:
- ambition versus personal cost;
- systems believed in versus systems criticized;
- public capability versus private difficulty;
- political aspiration versus frustration with old politics;
- rational/evidence-driven work alongside spiritual searching.

The scene must not become confessional spectacle. It exists to explain the next transformation.

### Scene 07 — Return / Jesse Cohen → StartOn
Purpose: one of the emotional peaks of the site.

The audience should experience the return before seeing the StartOn brand.

Scroll choreography:
1. childhood/place memory;
2. visual return to the neighborhood;
3. first-person realization;
4. mynet source / press photography;
5. broadcast frame;
6. physical StartOn model;
7. only then reveal STARTON as the name of the response.

Core line direction:
> יצאתי מכאן ילד שחיפש מקום. חזרתי כאדם שרוצה לבנות מקום לאחרים.

### Scene 08 — Creation / Music / Culture
Purpose: show that identity is larger than public service or politics.

Use official video frames and music surfaces. Motion should become more rhythmic and playful, without breaking the documentary identity.

### Scene 09 — Research / Making frameworks from lived questions
Purpose: show why research exists in the story rather than displaying an academic menu.

The reader sees the question first, then the framework or school of thought.

Knowledge Commons appears here contextually:
- competing schools;
- supporting and critical evidence;
- Igor's own frameworks clearly labeled as original frameworks, not academic consensus;
- practical takeaway for the reader.

### Scene 10 — 7YA / Building memory while still living
Purpose: reveal the system only after the person is understood.

7YA is framed as an answer to a human problem: platforms forget context, feeds fragment memory, and life becomes disconnected records.

Only here should more explicit system language become visible.

### Scene 11 — NOW / “I am still in the middle”
The current period is not a detached feed.

Recent verified posts, interviews, StartOn work, research, public activity, music and new 7YA material enter as the live edge of the autobiography.

Every current item answers:
**Why is this part of the story I am still becoming?**

### Scene 12 — YOU / Handoff
The story explicitly turns toward the visitor.

Direction:
> מספיק עליי. מה איתך?

The visitor can identify a current tension — belonging, money, failure, creation, service, parenting, opportunity, fear, building, public impact — and continue into the 7YA Companion / Growth Path.

The handoff must feel earned by the autobiography, not like a conversion funnel.

## 5. “Then / Now / Seven Years Ahead” Device

Use selectively at major turning points, not on every scene.

- **אז** — what Igor believed or understood then.
- **היום** — what he understands in 2026.
- **בעוד שבע שנים** — what he hopes this moment will have helped build.

The device should appear as a cinematic reflection layer rather than three repetitive cards.

## 6. Visual Direction

### 6.1 Overall
- premium documentary / autobiographical cinema;
- full-bleed source imagery;
- deep black / graphite as the theatrical base;
- warm paper / human archival tones for memory;
- restrained 7YA acid signal for active system states, not as a constant decorative color;
- typography alternates between large emotional editorial type and compact source metadata;
- fewer borders, grids and repeated boxed cards on the primary journey.

### 6.2 No collage rule
No collage compositions. When multiple sources are needed in one moment, they enter sequentially through motion, stacking, sliding or depth — not as a scrapbook collage.

### 6.3 Presence of Igor
The site must use multiple authentic visual states of Igor when source material allows: interview, public work, neighborhood, creation, music, speaking, candid/public-safe moments.

The same hero portrait must not become the default fallback across unrelated chapters.

## 7. Motion Language

Motion is categorized by narrative purpose:

### ARRIVAL
Slow parallax, geographic drift, subtle text reveal.

### RUPTURE
Harder cuts, frame interruption, temporary loss of visual continuity.

### DISCIPLINE / SERVICE
Measured linear transitions, constrained framing, minimal ornament.

### VOICE
Text or authored artifact enters from the lived scene.

### PROPAGATION
Original post remains the center while external nodes expand outward. Motion should show causality, not decorative particles.

### RETURN
Past visual and present visual align or crossfade into the same geography.

### BUILD
Fragments become structure: space, people, tools and project imagery resolve into StartOn / 7YA.

### NOW
Motion remains alive and slightly unresolved; no “final chapter” closure.

All animation must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Reduced-motion mode preserves the narrative order and content without animation dependence.

## 8. Companion Integration

The Companion remains a core product but changes its presentation.

Rules:
- immediately discoverable;
- does not cover the first-person cold open;
- knows the active autobiographical scene;
- suggested prompts are scene-native, e.g. “מה קרה כאן?”, “תראה לי את המקור”, “מה אתה מבין מזה היום?”, “איך זה קשור אליי?”;
- can open source/evidence context without claiming to be Igor;
- clearly identifies itself as AI;
- personal Growth Path data remains private according to the product's existing privacy model;
- on API failure, graceful fallback remains available.

## 9. Information Architecture After Redesign

The homepage is the cinematic autobiography.

Deep utility pages remain:
- Media / Public Record — full searchable media archive;
- Museum — deep visual archive;
- Research — frameworks and Knowledge Commons depth;
- Music — complete music/video room;
- Speaker — media/booking utility;
- Create / Growth — visitor action paths;
- Evidence / diagnostics — proof and integrity layers where relevant.

`LIFE / ECHO / LAB / BUILD` may remain as internal/system navigation language but must not dominate the autobiographical homepage above or between every scene.

## 10. Homepage Component Migration

Current production homepage composition:
- `LifeFirstHero`
- `PersonalChronology`
- `LifeGeography`
- `RightNow`
- `WorldRooms`
- impact stack: `PublicActionStage` + `InfluenceUniverse` + `PostPortraitWall`
- `StartOnRoom`
- `ResearchRoom`
- `CreateRoom`
- `UserHandoff`
- `DeepArchiveRiver`

Target migration:

### Replace / substantially rewrite
- `LifeFirstHero` → cinematic cold open.
- `PersonalChronology` → scene engine / autobiographical sequence.
- `RightNow` → final live autobiographical scene.
- `UserHandoff` → earned “YOU” scene connected to Companion.

### Absorb into chronological scenes
- `LifeGeography` → origin / return scenes.
- `PublicActionStage` → voice / public echo scenes.
- cinematic `InfluenceUniverse` → propagation sequences, with full index retained on deep pages.
- `PostPortraitWall` → source objects inside relevant scenes; searchable wall removed from homepage.
- `StartOnRoom` → return / building scene.
- `ResearchRoom` → research scene.
- `CreateRoom` → creation + user handoff where appropriate.

### Move out of primary journey
- `WorldRooms` as a large navigation block.
- `DeepArchiveRiver` as a large homepage warehouse. Replace with a restrained “go deeper” exit to archive/museum, or retain only a minimal epilogue strip.

This prevents the same public source from being repeated across several homepage modules.

## 11. Scene Data Model

Use a structured scene model, separate from rendering logic.

Suggested shape:

```ts
type AutobiographicalScene = {
  id: string;
  period: string;
  theme: 'origin'|'service'|'fatherhood'|'voice'|'echo'|'rupture'|'return'|'starton'|'creation'|'research'|'system'|'now'|'you';
  firstPerson: LocalizedText;
  reflection?: {
    then?: LocalizedText;
    now?: LocalizedText;
    sevenYears?: LocalizedText;
  };
  media: SceneMedia[];
  publicEcho?: EchoChain[];
  knowledge?: KnowledgeLens[];
  companionPrompts?: LocalizedText[];
  privacyLevel: 'public'|'public-safe';
};
```

Media objects must explicitly distinguish:
- documentary image;
- public video frame;
- post/source artifact;
- editorial reconstruction.

The renderer must not infer evidence status from visual appearance.

## 12. Source and Editorial Rules

- No unsupported biographical specificity.
- Resolve known factual conflicts before publishing the conflicting detail; otherwise use a verified broader formulation.
- Source-local metrics only; never sum platform reach unless a source itself reports a combined metric and that fact is clearly attributed.
- Owner-authored source ≠ independent verification.
- Public-source article ≠ partnership/endorsement.
- Illustrative reconstruction ≠ historical documentation.
- Children and third parties receive strict privacy protection.
- Financial, legal, relationship and family hardship may be referenced only where public-safe and necessary to the narrative; never expose third-party private details.
- Spiritual/Kabbalistic interpretation is labeled as spiritual/interpretive, not empirical evidence.
- Igor's original research frameworks are labeled as original frameworks, not consensus scholarship.

## 13. Multilingual Behavior

Hebrew is the editorial source language for this redesign.

English and Russian must preserve:
- first-person voice;
- emotional intensity;
- factual boundaries;
- source links and dates;
- privacy boundaries.

Translations should not introduce stronger claims than Hebrew.

## 14. Mobile Design

Mobile is not a compressed desktop dashboard.

Target rhythm:
**full-screen media → one first-person thought → source/echo reveal → continue.**

Rules:
- single dominant visual per viewport whenever possible;
- horizontal carousels only for clearly secondary source browsing;
- no tiny analytics dashboards;
- Companion reachable above the mobile dock without covering key text;
- touch targets ≥ 44 px;
- safe-area insets respected;
- videos load on explicit interaction when practical.

## 15. Performance and Accessibility

- Preserve semantic reading order even if media animates visually.
- Avoid autoplay audio.
- Lazy-load non-critical video embeds.
- Prefer poster frames until interaction.
- Maintain alt text that describes the source image without inventing context.
- Keyboard access for scene controls, source drawers and Companion.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` provides a complete non-animated path.
- New motion libraries are not introduced unless existing capabilities cannot implement the design without excessive custom complexity.
- Keep layout shift low by reserving media aspect ratios / dimensions.

## 16. Acceptance Criteria

The redesign is not accepted merely because tests are green.

### Emotional / editorial gate
A first-time visitor who spends five minutes on the homepage should be able to answer:
1. Where did Igor come from emotionally and geographically?
2. What changed him?
3. Why did public voice matter to him?
4. How did at least one personal publication travel into wider public space?
5. Why did StartOn emerge from the biography?
6. Why does 7YA exist now?
7. What can the visitor do next for themselves?

### Visual gate
- Igor or authentic life/source material dominates the visual field across the journey.
- No repeated generic fallback image across unrelated chapters.
- No major homepage chapter is primarily a grid of metadata cards.
- At least three key transitions use narrative scroll choreography rather than hover-only motion.
- Public propagation is visible as an actual visual sequence.

### Repetition gate
No primary source is presented as a full feature in more than one homepage scene unless repetition is narratively necessary and clearly transformed.

### Evidence gate
- Every public metric shown has a source and date context.
- No combined artificial reach total.
- Evidence status remains inspectable.
- Owner claims and independent sources remain distinguishable.

### Companion gate
- Companion is discoverable on first viewport / immediate interaction range.
- Active scene context is available to Companion.
- AI identity is explicit.
- API failure is graceful.

### Mobile gate
- cold open is emotionally legible on a phone;
- first three scenes work without tiny text or horizontal dashboard dependence;
- Companion remains usable above mobile navigation;
- media does not trap scroll.

## 17. QA Strategy

### Automated
- render/sanity test for the homepage;
- tests for scene order;
- no forbidden reader-facing system jargon in the cold open and early scenes;
- evidence objects preserve source/date/status fields;
- Companion scene context changes as the active scene changes;
- reduced-motion path renders all content;
- mobile nav + Companion collision test;
- source-link integrity for anchor scenes;
- API failure fallback for Companion.

### Visual
Capture desktop and mobile checkpoints for:
1. cold open;
2. origin;
3. service;
4. first propagation sequence;
5. StartOn return/reveal;
6. research;
7. NOW;
8. YOU / Companion handoff.

Visual QA is pass/fail against the emotional, repetition and source rules — not just pixel correctness.

## 18. Rollout Strategy

1. Build the new cinematic scene engine alongside the current homepage composition.
2. Populate only source-safe anchor scenes first.
3. Add propagation choreography and scene-aware Companion context.
4. Run automated + visual QA on desktop/mobile.
5. Replace the current homepage only after the new journey passes the acceptance gates.
6. Keep deep archive/media/research pages intact during the first homepage rollout unless a dependency requires a small compatibility change.
7. Reconcile verified production changes back into the canonical GitHub tree rather than leaving another AppDeploy-only divergence.

## 19. Explicit Non-Goals for First Release

- No attempt to recover every historical post before launch.
- No fabricated childhood imagery.
- No full 3D world or heavy WebGL requirement.
- No endless animation layer for its own sake.
- No rewrite of every deep utility page before the homepage works.
- No new social-login or data-collection system merely for the redesign.
- No exposure of private family material to increase emotional intensity.

## 20. Definition of Success

7YA succeeds when the visitor no longer thinks:

> “This is a sophisticated archive about Igor Vepretski.”

and instead feels:

> “I moved through part of this person's life, saw how pain became voice and building, and now I understand why this system exists — and why it is asking something of me too.”

That emotional transformation is the product.
