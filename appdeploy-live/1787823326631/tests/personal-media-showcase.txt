# Personal Hero + Media Proof QA Contract

## Test A - Hero tells the personal transformation without losing actionability
Viewport: mobile 375x667 and desktop 1280x800

Steps:
1. Open the Hebrew homepage at scroll position 0.
2. Confirm the candidate headline contains `מילד מוכה ללוחם ולמפקד` and the second line contains `מהישרדות — לאחריות`.
3. Confirm owner credit is visible near the hero media.
4. Confirm primary conversion actions remain usable and the hero does not become a text wall.
5. Repeat in English and Russian.

Expected:
- HE/EN/RU all communicate the same evidence-safe transformation arc.
- The hero remains visually dominated by Igor, not by UI chrome.
- Contact/evidence actions remain present and tappable.
- No direct quote marks are used for editorial bridging copy.
- The legacy `חייל מצטיין` wording is not present in the production candidate unless a direct source is added.

## Test D - External image failure degrades safely
Viewport: mobile 375x667

Steps:
1. Block one Google Drive thumbnail request and one external publisher image request.
2. Reload the homepage.
3. Inspect the affected cards.

Expected:
- Cards retain readable title, source, quote/statement and credit.
- Owner archive media may use an owner-safe local fallback.
- Third-party media never falls back to an Igor portrait or unrelated 7YA artwork; it becomes a labeled source poster with publisher and year.
- No infinite onError loop occurs.
- A media failure never removes the entire proof rail.

## Test E - Claim hygiene

Steps:
1. Inspect all quotation marks inside the media-proof component.
2. Verify quoted phrases are source-bound and short.
3. Verify editorial phrases such as `לפני החשיפה היה שירות` and `העבר לא מוצג כאן כמדליה. הוא מסביר את הדרך.` use the editorial statement style rather than quote styling.
4. Verify the mynet quote is `אני לא האמנתי בעצמי אבל מישהו האמין בי` and links to the mynet profile.
5. Verify the StartOn quote `„זכיתי” לשאת את התואר „נער בסיכון”` links to the official StartOn site.
6. Confirm no new reach, views, engagement or impact metrics are added by this component.

Expected:
- Direct quotes are traceable to the linked source or authored owner/public project material.
- Editorial copy is distinguishable from publisher quotations.
- No metric inflation or class mixing is introduced.
