# Master Entity Index — editorial release record

## Purpose

Create a public, visual and evidence-governed entity atlas for Igor Vepretski without treating a self-authored master index as independent proof.

## Public identity hierarchy

1. Igor Vepretski / איגור ופרצקי / Игорь Вепрецкий — canonical person identity.
2. StartOn — documented social mission founded by Igor.
3. 7YA — public organizing system for identity, content, evidence, AI and action.
4. Ido Vepretski — creative/discovery alias only; not a replacement canonical identity.

## Visual correction

The page uses at least five distinct visual sources:

- canonical Igor portrait;
- mynet press photography;
- StartOn television image;
- Russian-language podcast thumbnail;
- public music video thumbnail.

The sources are labeled and linked. They are not represented as original 7YA photography when they originate from external publishers.

## Claim controls

The following are not published as verified facts without direct evidence:

- exact university degrees, honors and study status;
- Gastocratic Governance as an established academic framework;
- multi-provider zero-downtime AI routing;
- active IPFS, Pinata and Solidity production pipeline;
- unverified music release names and credits;
- unverified podcast episode appearances;
- proposals or strategic drafts as completed public results.

## Privacy controls

The public page excludes partner, children and family identifiers. It also states that addresses, health, finances, legal matters, credentials, secrets and operational information must not be published.

## Acceptance gate

`scripts/check-entity-index.mjs` validates:

- canonical metadata and crawl controls;
- at least five distinct visual sources;
- verification-state labels for academic, technical and creative claims;
- absence of known private family identifiers;
- sitemap inclusion;
- homepage navigation link;
- non-thin visual CSS.
