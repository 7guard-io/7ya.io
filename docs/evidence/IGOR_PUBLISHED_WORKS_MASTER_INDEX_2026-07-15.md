## Publication workflow

1. Discover across English, Hebrew and Russian name variants and platform nodes.
2. Capture URL, publisher, title, date, language, asset type and dated metrics.
3. Resolve identity and distinguish owned, organization-owned, external amplification and unrelated records.
4. Classify the claim state: `VERIFIED`, `DOCUMENTED`, `SELF_ATTESTED`, `CONCEPT`, `PROTOTYPE`, `SOURCE_PENDING` or `PRIVATE`.
5. Quarantine conflicts and unsupported upgrades.
6. Add the candidate to the ledger and public museum with source and verification label.
7. Run route, link, artifact, entity, privacy, accessibility and structured-data gates.
8. Deploy one immutable source revision.
9. Verify the public domain serves the same revision and record the observed source SHA.
10. Monitor drift and append corrections without deleting history.
